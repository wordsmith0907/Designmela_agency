01

What Data We Collect

▾

We collect minimal information necessary to respond to client inquiries and scope design/dev projects:

  • Contact Form Inputs: Name, email address, phone number, project type, and brief description submitted via our main contact form or audit request modals.
  • Dee AI Chat Messages: Queries, pricing requests, and project briefs typed directly into the Dee chat widget.
  • Initiated Direct Messages: Contact details shared when initiating a WhatsApp chat from our site.
  • Technical Session Metadata: Basic browser metadata, regional currency selection, and IP location detection to display localized pricing.
02

Why We Collect It

▾

Your details are used solely to facilitate business communication and project delivery:

  • Responding to service inquiries and scheduling project discovery calls.
  • Preparing accurate project estimates, timelines, and proposal briefs.
  • Improving AI assistant (Dee) prompt relevance for agency services.
  • Fulfilling digital presence audit requests.

We never sell, rent, or trade client or visitor contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

03

WhatsApp Escalation & External Platforms

▾

Our site features direct integration with WhatsApp for fast inquiry escalation:

🟢 WhatsApp Hand-Off Terms

  • Clicking "Continue on WhatsApp" or secondary fallback contact links hands the chat off to WhatsApp's native mobile app or Web client.
  • Conversations conducted on WhatsApp take place on WhatsApp's external platform and are subject to WhatsApp's Privacy Policy, which operates outside Designmela's direct infrastructure control.
  • Both our Primary WhatsApp line (+91 80820 17828) and Secondary fallback line (+91 95993 20907) operate under these same external platform rules.
04

Data Storage & Session Retention

▾

We prioritize local-first data privacy for chat interactions:

💾 15-Minute Client-Side Session Storage

  • Browser Local Storage: Dee's active chat thread and temporary inquiry progress are stored locally in your browser's sessionStorage.
  • Automatic 15-Minute Expiry: Chat session state automatically expires and wipes completely after 15 minutes of inactivity or when the browser tab is closed.
  • Zero Unsolicited Server Logging: No chat transcript data is saved to remote databases or external servers unless you explicitly submit a contact form or initiate a WhatsApp message.
05

Third-Party Service Providers

▾

We rely on trusted third-party infrastructure providers to host and run our site services:

  • Vercel: Site hosting and serverless function routing.
  • Google Gemini API: Processing natural language queries for Dee AI chat responses.
  • Web3Forms: Secure submission delivery for contact forms and digital audits.
  • WhatsApp (Meta): Direct messaging and lead escalation.
06

Cookies & Local Storage

▾

We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies. We utilize standard browser sessionStorage strictly to maintain currency preferences and retain active chat history during your visit.
